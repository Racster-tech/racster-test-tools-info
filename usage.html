Usage information — Racster TEST mail reader

This tool is operated only by authorised Racster maintainers for synthetic test-email verification. It is not offered as a public email-reading service.

Connect only the designated test mailbox with the account owner’s consent. Use only the specific test-operation records allowed by the Racster workflow. Production customer messages and live payment activity are outside its purpose.

Google may require renewed authorisation if the owner revokes access or an access grant becomes invalid. The tool reports that condition; it does not bypass Google’s sign-in or security controls.
